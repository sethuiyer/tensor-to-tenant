# Sailboat Retrospective Template

The Sailboat is the weekly human ritual for Tensor-to-Tenant. It turns a
108-week apprenticeship from a checklist into a navigable journey.

```text
                 Rocks
           upcoming risks

Wind  →       [ Boat ]       →  Island
helpers       learner            capability

                 Anchor
             current drag
```

Use it at the end of every week. Keep the answers short and concrete; the goal
is to choose the next heading, not write a performance review.
